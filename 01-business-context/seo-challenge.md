# SEO Growth Challenge

## Business context

The company operates in the Dutch staffing and recruitment market. The website already generated organic traffic, but SEO was not yet being managed as a structured growth channel.At the start of the six-month period, organic traffic was approximately:

**~2,000 monthly visitors**

The opportunity was not simply to produce more content. The bigger opportunity was to improve how technical SEO, website structure, content and measurement worked together.

---

## The growth question

The main question was:

**How can we turn organic search into a scalable acquisition channel instead of treating SEO as a collection of individual optimisations?**

To answer that, we needed to look beyond rankings. We looked at the complete organic system:

**Technical foundation → Search demand → Site structure → Content → Visibility → Traffic → Conversion**

---

## Where we saw opportunity

The work focused on several areas:

- Technical SEO foundations
- Crawlability and indexation
- Website and page structure
- Search intent
- Content coverage
- Internal linking
- Landing-page quality
- Organic performance measurement

Rather than treating these as separate projects, we approached them as connected growth levers. A technical improvement has limited value if the right content does not exist. Content has limited value if search engines cannot properly discover and understand it. Traffic has limited commercial value if the landing experience does not support the next step.

---

## The objective

The objective was to create sustainable organic growth while improving the quality of the website foundation. We wanted to:

1. Increase relevant organic visibility
2. Attract more qualified organic traffic
3. Strengthen the technical SEO foundation
4. Improve the structure and discoverability of important pages
5. Build a more systematic approach to content
6. Improve how organic performance was measured and prioritised

The focus was therefore not: **More traffic at any cost** It was: **Build a stronger organic acquisition engine.**

---

## Result

Within six months, organic traffic grew from approximately:

**2,000 → 6,500 monthly visitors**

That is a **3.25x increase in organic traffic**. The result was created through a combination of technical, structural and content improvements executed together with the marketing team. The next sections of this repository break down how we approached each part of that system.
