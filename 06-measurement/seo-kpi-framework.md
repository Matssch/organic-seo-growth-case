# SEO KPI Framework

## Measuring more than rankings

Once the measurement foundation was in place, we wanted to avoid managing SEO through isolated metrics such as keyword rankings.

Rankings can be useful diagnostics, but they are not the final business outcome.

The measurement model therefore followed the organic journey:

**Indexation → Impressions → Clicks → Organic Traffic → Behaviour → Conversion**

Each stage answers a different question.

---

## 1. Indexation

The first question was:

> Can Google actually access and index the pages we want to grow?

This was especially important because approximately **70% of the website was not indexed** at the start of the project.

Indexation therefore became one of the first technical health indicators.

We looked at:

- Indexed pages
- Non-indexed pages
- Important pages missing from Google
- Changes after technical and structural improvements

This helped separate technical problems from content problems.

If a page was not indexed, improving its copy alone was unlikely to solve the real constraint.

---

## 2. Search visibility

Once important pages were discoverable, the next question became:

> Are these pages appearing for relevant searches?

Google Search Console gave us visibility into:

- Impressions
- Search queries
- Landing pages
- Changes in search visibility
- Organic clicks

Impressions became an early indicator that Google was beginning to connect our pages with relevant search demand.

The logic was:

**Indexed but no impressions**

may indicate a relevance, demand or page-quality problem.

---

## 3. Organic clicks

Visibility only becomes valuable when users choose to visit.

Clicks therefore helped us understand whether our search presence was actually attracting users.

We could evaluate areas such as:

- Pages generating the most organic clicks
- Queries driving traffic
- Pages gaining momentum
- Pages with visibility but limited click-through

This created opportunities to improve:

- Page titles
- Meta descriptions
- Search relevance
- Intent alignment

---

## 4. Organic traffic

Organic traffic became the clearest growth KPI for the project.

At the start:

**~2,000 monthly organic visitors**

Within six months:

**~6,500 monthly organic visitors**

That represents:

**3.25x the original monthly traffic level**

or approximately:

**+225% growth**

This was the main headline result.

But traffic was not reviewed in isolation.

We also needed to understand where that traffic entered the website and what happened next.

---

## 5. Landing-page performance

GA4 allowed us to move beyond the total organic traffic number.

We could increasingly review performance at landing-page level.

Questions included:

- Which pages attract organic users?
- Which page types are growing?
- Are important vacancy pages receiving traffic?
- Are users entering through relevant commercial or candidate journeys?
- Where are users leaving?

This helped us identify whether growth was concentrated in useful parts of the website or simply increasing total sessions.

---

## 6. User behaviour

Traffic is only useful if the landing experience works.

The next layer therefore looked at what users did after arriving.

Depending on the page, useful signals could include:

- Engagement
- Navigation to another relevant page
- Vacancy views
- CTA interactions
- Journey progression
- Conversion activity

The exact action depended on the purpose of the page.

A vacancy page and a company information page should not necessarily be evaluated against the same behaviour.

---

## 7. Conversion

The long-term objective was to connect organic acquisition more directly to business outcomes.

With GA4 and GTM properly implemented, we created a better foundation for measuring important actions across the website.

The measurement ambition became:

**Organic visitor**

↓

**Relevant landing page**

↓

**Meaningful interaction**

↓

**Conversion action**

↓

**Commercial outcome**

This allowed SEO to move closer to wider Growth and marketing measurement.

The traffic increase is the primary documented result in this case.

The project also created the tracking foundation needed to measure conversion more systematically going forward.

---

## The SEO KPI tree

The complete framework became:

### Technical health

- Indexation
- Broken pages
- Redirect health
- Website performance

↓

### Search visibility

- Impressions
- Queries
- Indexed landing pages

↓

### Acquisition

- Organic clicks
- Organic users
- Organic sessions
- Landing-page traffic

↓

### Behaviour

- Engagement
- Page progression
- CTA interaction

↓

### Conversion

- Applications
- Leads
- Other relevant conversion actions

↓

### Commercial value

- Qualified applications
- Qualified leads
- Revenue contribution

The further down the tree we could measure, the stronger the connection between SEO activity and business performance became.

---

## Diagnostic logic

The framework also helped us diagnose where performance was being lost.

| Signal | Likely question |
|---|---|
| Page not indexed | Is there a technical or structural problem? |
| Indexed, no impressions | Does the page match real search demand? |
| Impressions, few clicks | Is the search result relevant and compelling? |
| Clicks, weak engagement | Does the landing page answer the search intent? |
| Traffic, little conversion | Is the next step clear and commercially relevant? |
| Conversion, low quality | Are we attracting the right audience? |

This helped turn reporting into decision-making.

---

## Reporting principle

The objective was not to create the largest SEO dashboard possible.

We wanted reporting to answer:

1. Are we technically healthy?
2. Are we becoming more visible?
3. Are we attracting more relevant traffic?
4. Is that traffic entering the right journeys?
5. What should we improve next?

The most useful KPI is the one that changes the next decision.

---

## Headline outcome

Within six months:

**Organic traffic: ~2,000 → ~6,500 monthly visitors**

At the same time, the underlying measurement system became significantly stronger through the implementation of:

- Google Search Console
- Google Analytics 4
- Google Tag Manager

That gave the team a much stronger foundation for moving from:

**SEO activity**

toward:

**measurable organic growth.**
