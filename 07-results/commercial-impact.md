# Results & Commercial Impact

## The outcome
The objective of the project was to turn organic search from an underdeveloped website channel into a structured source of growth. Within six months, monthly organic traffic increased from approximately: **2,000 → 6,500 visitors**

That means the website reached: **3.25x the original organic traffic level** or approximately: **+225% growth**

The result was achieved by improving the complete SEO system rather than relying on one isolated tactic.

---

## Headline results
| Area | Starting point | After six months |
|---|---:|---:|
| Monthly organic traffic | ~2,000 | ~6,500 |
| Organic growth | Baseline | +225% |
| Website performance score | ~40 | ~88 |
| Search Console | Not properly implemented | Implemented |
| GA4 | Not properly implemented | Implemented |
| GTM | Not properly implemented | Implemented |
| Indexation | ~70% not indexed | Technical/indexation issues actively addressed |
| Website approach | Online company flyer | Search-led acquisition platform |
| Content approach | Company-focused | Search- and solution-focused |

The most important result was not one individual SEO metric. It was that several weak parts of the organic system improved at the same time.

---

## 1. Organic traffic became a meaningful acquisition channel
At approximately 2,000 monthly organic visitors, SEO already had some value but significant unused potential. Reaching approximately 6,500 monthly visitors changed the scale of the channel. The website was now bringing in more than three times the monthly organic audience it had at the start of the project. This created a much stronger foundation for:

- Vacancy discovery
- Candidate acquisition
- Service visibility
- Commercial landing pages
- Future content growth

Organic search became something we could actively manage and develop rather than simply observe.

---

## 2. The technical foundation became significantly stronger
The project also improved the website underneath the traffic numbers. At the start:

- Measurement was incomplete
- Approximately 70% of the website was not indexed
- Duplicate content existed
- Redirect issues required attention
- Website performance was weak
- Page-level SEO structures were inconsistent

We worked through these constraints together with the marketing team and web development. Website performance improved from approximately: **40 → 88**

This gave future SEO activity a much stronger technical foundation.

---

## 3. The website changed its role
One of the most important outcomes was strategic. The website moved away from functioning mainly as an online company flyer. Previously, the website largely started with: **Who are we and what do we do?**

The new approach increasingly started with: **What is the user searching for and what problem are they trying to solve?**

That shift affected:
- Website structure
- Vacancy visibility
- Content
- Page titles
- Headings
- URLs
- Internal linking
- Calls to action

The website became more aligned with external demand.

---

## 4. Content became part of acquisition
Content also changed from a communication activity into a growth lever. Instead of only explaining the organisation, we increasingly used content to answer actual search needs. The model became:

**Search demand** → **Relevant content** → **Useful landing page** → **Next step**

This created a stronger relationship between SEO, content and the wider customer or candidate journey.

---

## 5. Measurement became actionable
Before the project, SEO performance could not be analysed properly. Implementing:

- Google Search Console
- Google Analytics 4
- Google Tag Manager

changed that. We could move from assumptions toward a clearer understanding of:

- Search visibility
- Organic traffic
- Landing-page performance
- User behaviour
- Website journeys

That measurement foundation also made future optimisation more systematic.

---

## 6. SEO became a team capability
The project also changed how SEO was managed. It was no longer an isolated specialist activity. Marketing, content and web development worked together around shared organic growth priorities. My role was to lead the direction and prioritisation while staying close to execution. The operating model became:

**Identify opportunity → Prioritise → Implement → Measure → Improve**

This made organic growth repeatable rather than dependent on one-off SEO projects.

---

## What drove the result
The increase from approximately 2,000 to 6,500 monthly organic visitors cannot be attributed to one change. The growth came from improving multiple parts of the system together:

### Measurement
GSC, GA4 and GTM implementation.

### Technical SEO
Indexation, redirects, duplicate content, URLs and website performance.

### On-page SEO
Titles, meta descriptions, headings and page structure.

### Site architecture
Stronger relationships between pages and improved internal linking.

### Search intent
Moving from company-focused messaging toward actual user needs.

### Content
Improving existing pages and creating stronger answers to relevant demand.

### Team execution
Marketing and web development working against shared priorities.

---

## What I would not claim
The traffic increase is a documented outcome of the six-month period. However, this case does not claim that every additional visitor came from one specific intervention. Multiple changes were happening across the website at the same time. The value of the case is therefore not: One SEO trick produced 225% growth. It is:

**Building a stronger technical, structural and content system created the conditions for sustained organic growth.**

That distinction matters.

---

## Commercial interpretation
The most important commercial change was that the business had a substantially larger organic audience entering the website every month. The project also created a stronger infrastructure for measuring what happened after acquisition. The next maturity step would be to connect organic performance more deeply to:

**Applications → Lead quality → Customers → Revenue**

That would move SEO measurement from traffic contribution toward full commercial contribution.

---

## Core result
Within six months: **Organic traffic: ~2,000 → ~6,500 monthly visitors**

**Website performance: ~40 → ~88**

Alongside that growth, the team established a stronger technical foundation, a search-led website structure and a more systematic way of operating SEO. The result was not simply more traffic. It was a stronger organic growth engine.
