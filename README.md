# Organic SEO Growth Case

> How my team and I grew organic traffic from approximately 2,000 to 6,500 monthly visitors in six months.

## Overview

This case shows how we approached SEO as a growth system rather than a collection of isolated optimisations.

The business already had an established website and existing organic traffic, but there was significant room to improve the technical foundation, site structure, content coverage and measurement.

The goal was not simply to publish more content.

The goal was to create a stronger organic acquisition engine.

**Technical foundation → Search demand → Site structure → Content → Visibility → Traffic → Conversion**

Within six months, organic traffic grew from approximately:

**2,000 → 6,500 monthly visitors**

More than 3x the original level.

This repository breaks down the decisions, work and operating model behind that growth.

## My role

I led the SEO direction and prioritisation together with my marketing team.

My responsibility was to connect technical improvements, content, website development and measurement to a clear commercial growth objective.

The result was a team effort.

My role was to make sure we focused on the highest-impact opportunities rather than treating SEO as a list of disconnected tasks.

## Core principle

**SEO is a growth system.**

Technical SEO creates the foundation.

Content creates relevance.

Site architecture connects the two.

Measurement tells us what deserves attention next.
